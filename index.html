FAQ

Questions

Do I need to sign up?
No. Every completed trade counts automatically.
Do rejected trades count?
No. Only completed trades count. Rejected, cancelled or reversed trades don't.
Can I win more than once?
One prize per week, but you can win again in a later week.
Is there a ticket limit?
Yes, 50 tickets per user per week (₦5,000,000 traded). Tickets don't carry over to the next week.
How will I know if I won?
We'll notify you in the app, and the winners list appears on this page after each draw. Winners from earlier weeks stay available under "Winners from past weeks".
Does the draw run every week?
Yes. A new draw week starts every Thursday, and the draw calendar on this page shows the current and upcoming weeks.
